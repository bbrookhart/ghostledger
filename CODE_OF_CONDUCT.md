# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-driven research community. Participants should welcome good-faith critique, distinguish people from ideas, disclose uncertainty, and avoid overstating results.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, publishing credentials or sensitive data, encouraging unauthorized access, and using project channels to coordinate harm.

## Research integrity

Contributors must not fabricate results, conceal exclusions, relabel ordinary errors as sabotage, or present scripted-agent validation as model-propensity evidence.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project.
